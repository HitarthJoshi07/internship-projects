entry no. 01

A little about the handwriting

I started as a print designer, fell into code by accident, and never really left. For the last six years I've worked at the seam between the two — building interfaces that feel considered, not just functional.

I care about the small stuff: the weight of a shadow, the timing of a transition, the difference between a button that works and one that feels good to press.

These days I split my time between freelance product work and a slow, ongoing love affair with generative art on the side.
